What is Foodies Run Club?

We started this club to make fitness more fun and social. Every meetup includes an all-level run (or walk), then a group stop at a local food spot.

Beginners are welcome, there is no pace pressure, and every city can request a pop-up run.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, every meetup is free.

Do I need to be fast?

Nope. Walkers and all paces are welcome.

How do I request my city?

Use our request form and vote for your location.

How far do you run?

Usually 3-4 miles, but flexible each event.
